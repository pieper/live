Multi-Volume — CTACardio + Panoramix selftest · real data

A port of the slicer-wgpu Multi-Volume selftest: CTACardio and CTAAbdomenPanoramix, each with its own transfer function, composited in a single ray-march with Panoramix offset +200 mm along R — which the selftest puts on an interactive linear transform. So this demo has a classic transform gizmo on Panoramix: drag an R/A/S-coloured arrow to translate along that axis, a ring to rotate about it, or the white centre to translate in the view plane; drag empty space to rotate the camera. The gizmo is screen-constant size, shines through the volume, and fades whichever axis is degenerate (an arrow head-on, a ring edge-on) — the same conventions Slicer and other 3D gizmos use. All GPU-local: the volume re-places on one uniform update, no pipeline rebuild.  ·  SlicerLive
